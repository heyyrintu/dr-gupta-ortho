Modifications (scripts/build_knee.py → public/models/knee-model.json):
- Converted Z-up to Y-up and centred on the knee joint line.
- Smoothed the surfaces (Taubin filter, no shrinkage).
- Cut the distal femur (~9 mm) and proximal tibia (~8 mm) into separate pieces with flat saw-cut faces.
- Baked per-vertex ambient occlusion and quantised positions to 16-bit integers.
- Added procedurally modelled, illustrative knee-replacement implants (not a real product, not BodyParts3D data).
